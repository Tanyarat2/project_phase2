## Test case details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Test case ID | TC-SYS-03 |
| Title | Staff creates a new product; a guest customer submits a product review; staff approves the review; the customer sees the approved review |
| Objective | Verify that staff can create and publish a new product, and that a guest customer can submit a review with a star rating, review text, name, and email, while invalid submissions (no rating, invalid email) are rejected. Verify that the review is held for approval, that staff can approve it, and that the approved review is publicly displayed on the product page. |
| Preconditions | 1. The shop is accessible at http://157.85.102.236/shop/. 2. The WordPress Admin page is accessible at http://157.85.102.236/shop/wp-admin/. 3. The tester has the Group 02 staff account, and it can open Products > Reviews. 4. Product reviews are enabled. 5. Guest customers can submit reviews. 6. Reviews from new authors are held for approval. 7. The customer side is opened in a separate private/incognito window (guest, not logged in). |
| Test data | Run ID: `RUNID` = YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS (the actual test execution date and time). Product: `G02-Review-Test-Product-RUNID`, Product type: Simple product, Regular price: 350 THB, Stock management: enabled, Stock: 10. Rating: 5 stars. Review: `The product quality is excellent.` Customer name: `G02 SYS03Tester`. Email: `g02.sys03.RUNID@example.com` (new for every run, so the review is always held for approval). Invalid email: `g02-invalid-email`. Customer type: Guest. |
| Tested by and date | Thanyakarn Panyadee / 16 November 2026, 21:14:30 |
